### This is the official website for Battle For Whatever Thing (BFWT), a object show series I started in 2022 inspired by [Battle For Dream Island (BFDI)](https://bfdi.tv) made by Cary and Michael Huang.
Both media are under the "object show" genre, but BFDI is an animated web series, whereas mine is a comic series drawn on books and shared with my friends. I only started to upload the comics online since December of 2025.
